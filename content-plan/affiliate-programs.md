# Affiliate programs to apply for (product photos + commission)

Researched October 2026 across the 107 products in our roundups (details in `content-plan/image-research/`).

Only 4 brands publish product photos anyone may reuse (Tractive, Sure Petcare, World's Best Cat Litter; already on the site).
Every other brand shares photos with **approved affiliates and retailers**, usually through the affiliate network's
product feed or a brand asset library. One network account unlocks applications to many brands.

## Apply in this order

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Ask directly (one email each)

- Whisker (Litter-Robot): pr@whisker.com, public media kit but terms need written OK.
- Halo Collar: press zip is "editorial use only"; ask if affiliate reviews are OK.
- West Paw: media@westpaw.com (retailer photo library).
- earthbath, SpotOn: hi-res images "on request".

Suggested email: "Hi, I run mypetexperts.com, which recommends [product] in our guide to [topic] (link). May we use your
official product photo with credit 'Photo: [Brand]'? Happy to link back to your site."

## After approval

Add the photo to the product in the admin (Products → product → Product photo, Photo credit, Photo permission page),
or send the image/link and I'll add it.
