Yes, dogs can eat plain lettuce. The AKC says romaine and iceberg lettuce are not toxic to dogs. Wash it, chop it
into small pieces, and give only a little. Too much can upset your dog's stomach. Salad toppings and tomato plant
leaves are where the trouble starts.

## Is lettuce good for dogs?

Lettuce is a light, crunchy snack. The AKC notes that it has fiber and beta-carotene, which the body turns into
vitamin A. It is also mostly water.

That does not make it a must-have. Your dog gets what they need from a complete dog food. Think of lettuce as a
small extra for a dog that likes the crunch.

## How do I serve lettuce to my dog?

Keep it simple:

- **Rinse it well.** The AKC warns that lettuce can carry germs such as E. coli and listeria. These can make dogs and
  people in your home sick.
- **Chop it up.** Big pieces are harder to digest, especially for small dogs. Small bits are also easier to chew.
- **Serve it plain.** No dressing, salt, or seasoning.

## How much lettuce can a dog eat?

A little. The AKC says too much can cause diarrhea, because lettuce has a lot of fiber. It does not give an exact
amount, so start with a few small pieces and see how your dog does. If your dog has loose stool, stop.

## Other kinds of lettuce and salad

### Can dogs eat romaine, iceberg, or arugula?

Yes. The AKC lists romaine, iceberg, and arugula as non-toxic to dogs. Serve them the same way: washed, chopped, and
plain. Spinach is also non-toxic, but the AKC says it has oxalic acid, which can block calcium and may harm the
kidneys. Romaine or iceberg is the easier pick.

### Can dogs eat salad?

It is best not to share yours. The AKC says salads often have ingredients that are toxic to dogs, such as onions.
Dressing and other toppings add more risk. If you want to share, set aside a few plain leaves before you add
anything else.

### Can dogs eat lettuce and tomatoes?

Ripe tomatoes and lettuce can both be fine, but they need different care. The ASPCA says ripe tomato fruit is
non-toxic to dogs. The rest of the tomato plant is not. The ASPCA lists solanine as the toxic part of the plant.

Signs of tomato plant poisoning listed by the ASPCA include:

- Drooling a lot
- Not wanting to eat
- Severe stomach upset
- Feeling down or weak
- Wide pupils
- A slow heartbeat

For a safe mix, give a few plain lettuce pieces and a bit of ripe tomato with the stem and leaves removed. Keep dogs
away from tomato plants in the garden. The ASPCA page does not say how much of the plant is harmful. That is why a
call to a vet is the right move.

## What if my dog ate a lot of lettuce?

A big pile of lettuce will most likely cause an upset stomach or diarrhea, because of the fiber. Offer water and
watch your dog. If the diarrhea does not stop, or your dog seems weak, call your vet.

If the lettuce came in a salad, check what else was in it. Onions are one example the AKC gives of a salad
ingredient that is toxic to dogs. If you are not sure, call your vet or a poison hotline.

## Other crunchy treats to try

If your dog likes the crunch, you could also try small pieces of plain [apple](/can-dogs-eat-apples/), without the
core or seeds. Treats of any kind should be no more than about 10% of your dog's daily calories. For the full list
