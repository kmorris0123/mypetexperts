Dogs can eat salmon when it is fully cooked, plain, and free of bones. **Raw or undercooked salmon is not safe.** It can carry a parasite that spreads a bacteria that causes salmon poisoning disease. This illness can kill a dog without treatment. Offer cooked salmon once in a while, not as a main meal.

## Why is raw salmon dangerous for dogs?

Raw salmon can carry tiny flukes (flat parasites). Those flukes can carry a bacteria called *Neorickettsia helminthoeca*. When a dog eats the fish, the bacteria get into the dog's body. The Merck Veterinary Manual says this causes salmon poisoning disease, even though the name sounds like a toxin.

Dogs can also get it from raw or undercooked trout. The Merck Veterinary Manual says it is found along the Pacific coast of the United States, mostly from northern California to Puget Sound. It also occurs inland along the routes that fish travel.

## What are the signs of salmon poisoning disease?

Signs usually start about 5 to 7 days after a dog eats infected fish. Merck says it can take up to 33 days. Look for:

- High fever
- Weakness and no interest in food
- Vomiting, and sometimes bloody diarrhea
- Weight loss
- Swollen lymph nodes
- Dehydration

Merck says up to 90% of dogs that get no treatment die within 7 to 10 days. Vets treat it with antibiotics and supportive care, such as fluids. Dogs treated early do much better.

## How do I serve salmon to my dog safely?

The AKC gives these steps:

1. **Cook it fully.** The AKC says to reach an inside temperature of 140°F.
2. **Take out every bone.** Salmon has small, brittle bones. They can choke a dog or hurt its gut.
3. **Keep it plain.** Bake, steam, or grill it with no seasoning, oil, or sauce.

Don't rely on freezing to make raw salmon safe. The Merck Veterinary Manual does not list freezing as a way to prevent the disease. It says the only way to prevent it is to keep dogs from eating raw or undercooked fish.

## How much salmon can a dog eat?

The AKC says to give prepared salmon no more than once a week. It should not be the main part of your dog's diet. Dog food made with salmon is fine every day, according to the AKC. The AKC also notes that salmon has omega-3 fats, which help the immune system, skin, and coat.

Our sources give no set amount per dog. Ask your vet how much fits your dog's size and health. As with any treat, keep extras to about 10% of your dog's daily calories.

## Other kinds of salmon

### Is smoked salmon okay for dogs?

No. The AKC says to avoid smoked fish products. Skip smoked salmon, and stick to plain cooked salmon instead.

### Can dogs eat canned salmon?

Yes, if you pick carefully. The AKC suggests low-sodium canned salmon packed in water. Check the label for added salt, oil, or seasoning.

### Is raw salmon ever okay?

No. The AKC and Merck both warn against raw salmon. If your dog eats some, call your vet the same day.

### What about salmon bones?

Take all bones out first. The AKC says they are small and brittle. They can choke your dog or hurt its stomach and gut.

## What if my dog ate raw salmon?

Call your vet right away and tell them how much your dog ate and when. Don't wait for signs. The AKC says owners should contact their vet right away. It is also worth telling the vet where the fish came from, since the disease is mostly found along the Pacific coast.

## Other fish to avoid

The AKC says to avoid fish that are high in mercury, such as tuna, swordfish, and king mackerel. For more on what to keep away from your dog, see our list of [foods that are toxic to dogs](/foods-toxic-to-dogs/).
