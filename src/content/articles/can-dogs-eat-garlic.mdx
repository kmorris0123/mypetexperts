No, dogs should not eat garlic. It is poisonous to dogs in every form: raw, cooked, baked, or powdered. Garlic harms
a dog's red blood cells and can lead to anemia, which means the blood can't carry enough oxygen. The AKC says no amount
should be considered safe.

## Why is garlic bad for dogs?

Garlic belongs to the Allium family, along with onions, chives, and leeks. Pet Poison Helpline says all of these are
poisonous to dogs. Garlic contains sulfur compounds that damage red blood cells. The damaged cells break apart,
and the dog becomes anemic.

Garlic is stronger than onion. The Merck Veterinary Manual says it is about 3 to 5 times more toxic. Dried and
powdered garlic is the most concentrated. That means a little of it holds a lot of garlic.

## Does cooking make garlic safe for dogs?

No. Merck lists raw, cooked, and dried garlic as toxic. The AKC adds that garlic baked into bread or mixed into sauces
still has the compounds that harm dogs.

## What signs show a dog ate garlic?

The first signs are often vomiting or diarrhea within hours. The blood damage comes later. The AKC says serious signs
can show up 2 to 5 days after eating, and Merck says signs usually appear a few days after the garlic was eaten.
Watch for:

- Tiredness or weakness
- Not wanting to eat
- Pale or yellow gums
- Fast breathing or a fast heartbeat
- Red or dark urine
- Collapse

## What should I do if my dog ate garlic?

Call your vet or a hotline right away. Tell them what your dog ate, about how much, and when. Your dog's size
matters, and so does the form of garlic. Vets work fastest when the garlic was eaten recently. Merck says treatment
starts by getting the garlic out of the stomach, and it can include oxygen, a blood transfusion, and IV fluids for
dogs that become very sick.

## Other kinds of garlic food

### Can dogs eat garlic bread?

No. Garlic bread has garlic baked in, and baking does not remove the harm. It is also full of butter and oil. The
AKC says a small amount, such as a bite of garlic toast, rarely causes life-threatening problems. Even so, don't give
it, and call your vet if your dog ate more than a bite.

### Can dogs eat food with garlic in it?

No. This covers pasta sauce, pizza crust, soup, stir-fry, and meat that was cooked with garlic. The AKC says dogs
should not eat garlic in any form, including as an ingredient in other foods. Check labels, too. Garlic powder is
common in seasoning mixes and snacks.

### Can dogs have chicken broth with garlic?

No. Broth with garlic in it is still a garlic food. Plain chicken broth is a different question, and it may also be
salty. If a recipe lists garlic or garlic powder, keep it away from your dog.

## How can I keep my dog away from garlic?

- Keep garlic bread, leftovers, and plates out of reach.
- Don't feed table scraps from savory meals.
- Read the labels on broth, sauces, and treats.
- Don't use garlic as a home flea remedy.

## What can I give my dog instead?

Plain, dog-safe foods are better choices. Try a few blueberries or a small piece of cooked chicken with nothing added.
Treats should be no more than about 10% of your dog's daily calories. See our guide to
[foods that are toxic to dogs](/foods-toxic-to-dogs/) for more things to keep out of reach.
