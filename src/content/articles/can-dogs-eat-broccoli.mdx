Yes, dogs can eat broccoli. Plain, cooked or raw, it is fine as an occasional treat in small pieces. The florets can upset
some dogs' stomachs, so start with a tiny bite. **Too much is a problem,** and the stalks can be a choking risk.

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
  - Serve it plain, with no butter, oil, salt, or garlic.
  - Cut it small. Cooked is gentler than raw.
  - Keep it small. The AKC says broccoli should stay under 10% of what your dog eats in a day.
  - Stop and call your vet if your dog gets a sore stomach or chokes.
</Callout>

## Why is broccoli okay for dogs?

Broccoli is high in fiber and vitamin C and low in fat, according to the American Kennel Club (AKC). That makes it a light
snack. It is not a replacement for dog food. The AKC says fruits and vegetables should add to a balanced diet, not
replace complete dog food.

## Why can broccoli upset a dog's stomach?

The florets (the "tree tops") contain natural compounds called isothiocyanates. The AKC says these can cause mild to
serious stomach irritation in some dogs. Some dogs react more than others, so watch yours.

## How much broccoli can a dog eat?

The AKC's chief veterinarian says broccoli is considered safe if it makes up less than 10% of your dog's daily food.
The AKC also says that more than 25% is toxic. The right amount for your dog depends on their size, so a small dog gets
much less than a big one. Most dogs only need a few small pieces.

## How do I serve broccoli to my dog?

- **Keep it plain.** The AKC says no seasonings and no oils.
- **Cut it into small, bite-size pieces.** This helps prevent choking.
- **Think about cooking it.** The AKC says dogs can have it raw or cooked, but says cooked broccoli helps avoid problems with
  tough stalks. Steamed or boiled with nothing added works well.
- **Start tiny.** Give one small piece. Wait and see how your dog does before giving more.
- **Skip hard stalks.** They have blocked the throat tube in some dogs, especially small ones.

## Other kinds of broccoli

### Is raw broccoli okay for dogs?

Yes, in small pieces. The AKC says dogs can eat it raw. The AKC says cooked is the better choice, since it helps avoid problems with tough pieces.

### Is cooked broccoli okay for dogs?

Yes, if it is plain. Steam or boil it with no butter, oil, salt, or spices.

### Are broccoli stalks okay for dogs?

Only with care. The AKC says tough stalks have caused blockages in the esophagus. Chop them very small, or leave them out.

## What if my dog ate too much broccoli?

A big helping of broccoli can upset the stomach. The AKC does not list exact signs, but vomiting, diarrhea, or a
tummy that seems sore are the usual signs of a stomach upset. Call your vet if your dog ate a lot, seems sick, or
is gagging or choking.

Call your vet right away if the broccoli had garlic or onion on it. The ASPCA says garlic and onion can irritate the
stomach and harm red blood cells, which can lead to anemia (a low number of red blood cells). For poison help, you can
call these hotlines:

- ASPCA Animal Poison Control: [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435)
- Pet Poison Helpline: [(855) 764-7661](tel:+18557647661)

Both may charge a fee. Don't make your dog vomit unless a vet tells you to.

## Other treats to try

Many dogs like other plain, cut-up fruits and vegetables. Try [apple slices](/can-dogs-eat-apples/) (no core or seeds)
or a bit of [banana](/can-dogs-eat-bananas/). Check our list of [foods that are toxic to dogs](/foods-toxic-to-dogs/) before you share something
new. Keep all treats to a small part of your dog's day, and talk to your vet before you change your dog's diet.
