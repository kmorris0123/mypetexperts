**Turkey bacon is not a good treat for cats.** Vet sources warn about salty and fatty human food, and turkey bacon can be both. One small bite by accident is unlikely to hurt a healthy cat. Don't offer it on purpose, and never make it a habit.

<Callout type="warn" title="When to call a vet">
  Call your vet if your cat ate a lot of turkey bacon, or if your cat throws up, seems very tired, or stops eating. For
  advice on a possible poisoning, you can also call a hotline. Both may charge a fee.

  - ASPCA Animal Poison Control: [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435)
  - Pet Poison Helpline: [(855) 764-7661](tel:+18557647661)

  Don't try to make your cat vomit unless a vet tells you to.
</Callout>

## Why is turkey bacon a poor choice for cats?

We could not find a vet source that talks about turkey bacon by name. So this advice comes from what the sources say
about fat and salt, which turkey bacon often has. Check the package to see how much salt it has.

**Fat.** Cornell's Feline Health Center lists "excessive dietary fat" as one possible factor linked to pancreatitis in
cats. Pancreatitis is a painful swelling of the pancreas, an organ that helps with digestion. Cornell also says the
cause is not fully known, and the illness is rare. The ASPCA also warns that very fatty foods can cause vomiting,
diarrhea, and pancreatitis in pets.

**Salt.** The ASPCA says too much salt can make pets very thirsty and make them pee more. In bad cases it can cause
shaking, seizures, and death. The Merck Veterinary Manual says salt poisoning is tied to water. It is unlikely when
fresh water is always available.

**Cats need cat food.** Cats must eat meat, but the Merck Veterinary Manual says their food needs special nutrients,
such as taurine, that are not in human food. Cornell says treats are not complete meals and should be only a small part
of the day.

## How much turkey bacon is too much?

No source we read gives a safe amount of turkey bacon for cats, so we won't give one. If your cat gets a tiny crumb by
accident, watch your cat for the rest of the day. Make sure fresh water is always out.

## What are the signs of trouble?

Watch for these after your cat eats a salty or fatty food:

- Throwing up or loose stool
- Acting tired or weak
- Not wanting to eat
- Drinking much more than usual

The Merck Veterinary Manual lists lethargy, loss of appetite, dehydration, vomiting, and belly pain as signs of
pancreatitis in cats. Some cats show no signs at all. These signs also happen with many other illnesses, so only a vet
can tell what is wrong.

Pet Poison Helpline lists stumbling, confusion, shaking, seizures, and collapse as signs of serious salt poisoning. If
you see these, get emergency help right away.

## What should I do if my cat ate turkey bacon?

1. **Take away the rest** so your cat can't eat more.
2. **Offer fresh water.** Don't force your cat to drink.
3. **Watch your cat** for the signs above.
4. **Call your vet** if your cat ate a lot, has any of those signs, or already has health problems.

Cornell says to see a vet if a cat that normally has a good appetite stops eating, even for one day.

## What does uncooked bacon mean for cats?

The ASPCA says raw meat can carry bacteria such as Salmonella and E. coli. These can make pets and people sick. Merck
and Cornell also say raw meat is not recommended for cats. Always cook meat first, and keep raw packages away from your
cat.

## What can I give my cat instead?

Your cat's regular cat food is the best choice. Cornell says treats should not be more than 10 to 15 percent of a cat's
daily calories. For something from the kitchen, plain cooked turkey without salt, spices, or bones is a better pick.
