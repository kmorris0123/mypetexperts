## How to choose a dog life jacket

### Who needs one

The AKC says any dog doing water activities can use a life jacket, whatever their swimming skill. It lists dogs with stocky bodies or short muzzles, lean dogs such as Whippets, puppies, senior dogs and dogs facing strong currents as ones that may need extra help.

### Jacket or vest?

The AKC says jackets give more flotation and coverage, so they suit boating and deep water. Vests are lighter and suit casual swimming, like a pool.

### Features that matter

- **A handle** so you can lift your dog quickly.
- **A D-ring** to attach a leash when your dog enters or leaves the water.
- **Bright color or reflective trim** so you can see your dog from far away and in low light.
- **Adjustable straps** for a snug fit.

### Sizing and fit

Measure your dog's neck, rib cage and hips, and body length from the base of the head to the tail. Some jackets go by weight instead, so use the size chart for the exact model. Check that your dog can swim level, sit and lie down. You should be able to slide two fingers under the straps.

## Safety tips

- **Test it first.** The AKC suggests a bathtub float test for small dogs. For larger dogs, clip on a leash or harness and try it in shallow water. Check movement, float and fit when the jacket is wet.
- **Introduce it at home.** The AKC suggests letting your dog sniff it and giving treats before you strap it on.
- **Stay close.** Outward Hound warns not to leave your dog alone while they wear its jacket, and to replace it if it is damaged.
- **Use rescue handles only in emergencies,** per Outward Hound.
- **Know your dog's limits.** The AKC says not every dog is suited to the water.
