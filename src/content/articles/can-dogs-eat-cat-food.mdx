A few bites of cat food usually won't seriously harm your dog. But **cat food is not made for dogs**, and it should not be
a regular meal. Eating it often can lead to upset stomach, weight gain, and a painful belly problem called pancreatitis.

<Callout type="info" title="Quick version">
  - Your dog stole a few bites? Watch them. Mild vomiting or loose stool can happen.
  - Your dog ate a lot, or got into a whole bag or many cans? Call your vet for advice.
  - Your dog has belly pain, keeps throwing up, or acts weak? Call your vet right away.
</Callout>

## Why do dogs love cat food?

The AKC explains that cat food has much more meat-based protein than dog food. That strong meat smell and taste is why
many dogs go straight for the cat's bowl.

## Why is cat food bad for dogs?

Cats and dogs need different diets. The AKC says cats are obligate carnivores, meaning they need meat to live. Dogs
are omnivores and need a more varied diet. Cat food is built for the cat's needs, not the dog's.

According to the AKC, a dog who eats cat food regularly may get:

- Stomach upset
- Extra weight
- Pancreatitis, an inflammation of the pancreas
- Extra strain on the liver and kidneys, because of the high protein

Some dogs are at more risk than others. If your dog has a health problem, such as pancreatitis or kidney or liver
disease, ask your vet before you let them near cat food.

## What is pancreatitis, and can cat food cause it?

The pancreas is an organ that helps your dog digest food. When it becomes inflamed, it hurts. The Merck Veterinary
Manual says the cause is often not clear. But it says eating trash, lots of table scraps, or other food a dog should
not eat is believed to be a common risk factor.

The AKC lists pancreatitis as a possible result of eating cat food. We did not find a source that says how much cat
food it takes to cause it. Most dogs who sneak a little will not get it.

## What signs should I watch for?

The AKC lists these signs of pancreatitis:

- Belly pain, sometimes with a hunched back
- Tiredness and weakness
- Not wanting to eat
- Vomiting or diarrhea
- A swollen belly
- Fever

The Merck Veterinary Manual adds that milder cases may show only a lost appetite, tiredness, and diarrhea. Pancreatitis
needs quick care from a vet. Merck says mild cases usually do well, but severe cases can have a poor outcome.

## What should I do if my dog ate cat food?

A small amount may cause only mild vomiting or diarrhea, says the AKC, though dogs react differently.

1. Take the food away and note how much your dog ate.
2. Watch your dog for vomiting, diarrhea, belly pain, or tiredness.
3. Call your vet if your dog seems sick, ate a large amount, or has any of the signs above.

Tell the vet about any health problems your dog has.

## Other kinds of cat food

### Is wet or canned cat food okay for dogs?

The same answer applies. The sources we read do not say wet food is better or worse than dry for dogs. A few licks or
bites are unlikely to be a problem. Regular meals of canned cat food carry the same risks the AKC describes for cat
food in general.

### Is dry cat food okay for dogs?

Again, a few pieces of kibble are unlikely to hurt. Dry cat food is still cat food. It is not balanced for dogs, so it
should not replace your dog's own food.

### Can puppies eat cat food?

Puppies should not eat cat food as a meal. The Merck Veterinary Manual says puppies need special puppy food that gives
enough calories, fat, protein, vitamins, and minerals for growth. It suggests looking for a label from AAFCO, a group
that sets pet food standards. Cat food is not made to meet a growing puppy's needs.

A puppy who steals a bite should be fine, but watch for vomiting or diarrhea and call your vet if it keeps going.

### Can a dog die from eating cat food?

The sources we read do not describe deaths from eating cat food. The risk is pancreatitis, which Merck says can be
very serious in severe cases. Most dogs who sneak some will not get that sick. If your dog shows signs of illness, call
your vet.

## How can I keep my dog out of the cat food?

The AKC suggests storing cat food where your dog cannot get it, or changing where you feed your cat. Some ideas:

- Feed your cat in a room your dog cannot enter.
- Put the cat's bowl up high, on a shelf or counter your dog cannot reach.
- Pick up the bowl after your cat is done.
- Store bags and cans in a closed cabinet.

## What should my dog eat instead?

The AKC says the best food for your dog is dog food, unless your vet tells you something different. Pick a food with an
AAFCO label that says it is complete and balanced for your dog's life stage. If your dog needs extra protein or a special
diet, ask your vet instead of switching to cat food.
