## How to choose a dog car harness

### Look for crash-test details

Kurgo says no government agency sets safety standards for dog travel products. That means the brand's word is only as good as its testing. Look for a named standard, like the child car seat standard Kurgo uses, or an independent rating. The Center for Pet Safety says its certification means the maker meets independently developed standards, and the group says it takes no money from pet product makers. Its certified products page lists carriers and kennels but no harnesses, so judge a harness rating by the brand's own page.

### Pick the right size

Measure your dog before you buy. Ruffwear sizes by girth around the widest part of the rib cage and says to pick the smaller size if your dog is between sizes. Kurgo sizes the Tru-Fit by chest and neck and says it should fit snug but not tight. Check the weight range too, since Kurgo's Tru-Fit crash rating stops at 75 lbs.

### Check how it connects

The AKC says dog seat belts work like the ones people use, and that a car harness attaches to the harness, not the collar. These picks all connect to the car's seat belt. Sleepypod includes a clip and buckle cover, and Kurgo's Tru-Fit includes a 10-inch tether with a steel carabiner.

### Decide if you want a walking harness too

Sleepypod's and Kurgo's Tru-Fit harnesses can also be used on walks. Kurgo's Impact is for car travel only.

## Safety tips

- **Put your dog in the back seat.** The AKC says dogs are best in the back, where air bags cannot hurt them.
- **Restrain your dog every ride.** Kurgo says a harness on a seat belt tether keeps a dog from becoming a projectile in a crash.
- **Keep dogs off laps and out of the front.** Kurgo lists this as the first step to cut driver distraction.
- **Keep windows up enough.** The AKC says dogs should not put their heads out the window of a moving car, and that rear windows should stay closed because dogs can jump out.
- **Follow the maker's setup steps.** Sleepypod warns against using non-Sleepypod accessories with its harnesses.
- **Think about the rest of the back seat.** A cover can keep hair off the upholstery but does not hold your dog in. See our guide to the [best dog car seat covers](/best-dog-car-seat-covers/).
