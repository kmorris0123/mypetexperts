Cottage cheese is not a good treat for cats. Many cats are lactose intolerant, which means dairy can give them
diarrhea or an upset stomach. Cats do not need dairy at all. **If your cat licked a little plain cottage cheese, it is
unlikely to be an emergency. Just do not make it a habit.**

## Why is cottage cheese not a good treat for cats?

### Many cats cannot digest dairy well

Cornell Feline Health Center says many cats are lactose intolerant. Lactose is the sugar in milk. A cat that cannot
digest it well can get stomach and bowel problems. The ASPCA says the same thing in simple terms: pets have little
lactase, the body's tool for breaking down milk sugar. Dairy can cause diarrhea or other upset.

The sources we read do not say exactly how much lactose is in cottage cheese or how much is too much. Cats also differ.
One cat may be fine, and another may get sick from a small bite.

### Cats are meat eaters

Cornell says cats are obligate carnivores. That means they need nutrients that come only from animal products. The Merck
Veterinary Manual says cats eat mainly meat, and that raw meat and dairy products of any kind should not be fed to
cats. A complete cat food already has what your cat needs, so cottage cheese adds nothing useful.

## What should I watch for if my cat ate cottage cheese?

Watch your cat for the next day or so. The main signs of a dairy problem are:

- Loose stool or diarrhea
- Vomiting
- A sore or upset belly

Most mild upsets pass on their own. Call your vet if the signs are strong, last more than a day, or your cat stops
eating or seems very tired. Call sooner for a kitten or a cat with a health problem.

## Is flavored cottage cheese safe for cats?

Check the label before you share anything. Some cottage cheese is mixed with other foods or seasonings. The Merck
Veterinary Manual says cats are the most sensitive animals to onion and garlic, and it says the problem has happened in
cats after eating less than a teaspoon of cooked onion. The ASPCA also lists chives with onions and garlic as foods that
can damage red blood cells. Skip any cottage cheese with onion, garlic, or chives.

The ASPCA also warns that too much salt can make pets sick. Plain foods are the better choice for any treat.

## Can cats eat cream cheese?

Cream cheese is also dairy, so the same advice applies. It is not a good treat for cats. Merck says dairy products should
not be fed to cats, and the ASPCA says dairy can cause diarrhea or other upset. A small lick is unlikely to be an
emergency, but there is no reason to offer it. Check the label on flavored kinds, such as ones with onion, garlic, or
chives.

## What can I give my cat instead?

The easiest treat is one made for cats. You can also use a few pieces of your cat's regular food as a reward. Cornell
says treats should not be more than about 10 to 15 percent of your cat's daily calories. Merck also warns that
overfeeding can lead to obesity and other serious health problems.
