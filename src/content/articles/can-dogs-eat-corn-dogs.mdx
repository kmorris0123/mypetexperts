No, corn dogs are not a good snack for dogs. **One small bite is unlikely to hurt a healthy dog.** But a corn dog is
fried, fatty, and salty, and the stick can be dangerous if your dog swallows it. If your dog got a whole one, call your
vet for advice.

<Callout type="warn" title="If your dog swallowed the stick, or ate a whole corn dog">
  Call your vet or an emergency clinic. Cornell calls a blockage in the gut an emergency that often needs surgery.
  You can also call a poison hotline about the food:

  - ASPCA Animal Poison Control: [(888) 426-4435](tel:+18884264435)
  - Pet Poison Helpline: [(855) 764-7661](tel:+18557647661)

  Both may charge a consultation fee. Don't make your dog vomit at home unless a vet tells you to.
</Callout>

## Why aren't corn dogs good for dogs?

A corn dog is a hot dog in a thick fried coating. That brings three problems.

**Fat.** The AKC says a high-fat diet is one of the most common triggers for pancreatitis. That is a painful swelling
of the pancreas, a small organ near the stomach. In some dogs, one large serving of fatty food is enough. The Merck
Veterinary Manual says that eating trash or a lot of table scraps is a common risk. Sources we read don't name hot
dogs or corn dogs, but they are fatty food. Some breeds are more at risk, such as Miniature Schnauzers, Yorkshire
Terriers, and Dachshunds.

**Salt.** The ASPCA says too much salt can cause thirst and peeing more, and sometimes a change in the body's minerals.
In bad cases it can cause vomiting, diarrhea, tremors, and seizures. Pet Poison Helpline says salty snacks are one
source of salt poisoning in pets. One bite is not likely to cause this. But a whole corn dog, or a few, is more salt
than a dog needs.

**Hidden seasonings.** The ASPCA says onions and garlic can damage red blood cells and cause anemia. Merck says dried
and powdered forms are the most concentrated. We can't say what is in any one brand of corn dog, so check the label.

## Is the corn dog stick dangerous?

Yes, it can be. The stick is the part owners forget. A dog that gulps a corn dog may swallow the stick too.

Cornell says a swallowed object that gets stuck in the gut is an emergency. It lists bones and corn cobs among the
things that commonly cause blockages. The page doesn't talk about corn dog sticks, so we can't say how often they cause
problems. But it is wise to treat any swallowed stick as a reason to call your vet.

Cornell lists these signs of a blockage:

- Vomiting
- Not wanting to eat
- Belly pain
- Diarrhea
- Dehydration, which means not enough water in the body
- Tiredness

Most blockages need surgery, and some can be treated with a camera put down the throat. Don't wait for signs to appear
before you call. Cornell says early care matters.

## What signs of pancreatitis should I watch for?

Watch for these after your dog eats a fatty food like a corn dog, according to the AKC and Merck:

- Throwing up, sometimes many times
- Not wanting to eat
- A sore or swollen belly, or a hunched back
- Diarrhea
- Weakness or tiredness
- Dehydration

Signs can show up within hours or days, and some mild cases show few signs. Call your vet if you see several of these.
The AKC says to get care right away if your dog won't drink, can't keep water down, or gets weaker. Early care helps
prevent problems, says Merck.

## What should I do if my dog ate a corn dog?

1. **Take away the rest and look for the stick.** Count the pieces so you know whether a stick is missing.
2. **Check the label** or ask the seller what is in it.
3. **Call your vet** if your dog ate a whole corn dog, may have swallowed the stick, or has a health problem such as
   pancreatitis in the past.
4. **Watch your dog** for the signs above for the next few days.

## Other kinds of corn dogs

### Can dogs eat mini corn dogs?

Better not. They are made the same way as full-size ones. Their small size makes it easier for a dog to swallow one
whole, stick and all.

### Can dogs eat plain hot dogs?

It is best to skip them. A hot dog has the same fat and salt, without the stick. If you want to use one for training, ask your vet first,
especially if your dog has had pancreatitis or other health problems.

### Can dogs eat the corn dog batter?

It is not poisonous on its own, but it is fried and greasy and gives your dog nothing it needs. Our guide on
[whether dogs can eat corn](/can-dogs-eat-corn/) has more about corn foods.

## What can I give my dog at the fair instead?

Bring a treat made for dogs, or a few pieces of your dog's own food, to hand out when you eat. Treats of any kind should
be a small part of what your dog eats in a day. For a longer list of foods to keep away from your dog, see our guide to
[foods that are toxic to dogs](/foods-toxic-to-dogs/).
