Yes, dogs can eat pineapple. A few bites of fresh, raw pineapple are a fine treat when you remove the spiky skin and the
tough core and cut the flesh into small pieces. Too much can upset your dog's stomach because it has a lot of sugar and
fiber.

## Is pineapple safe for dogs?

Fresh pineapple flesh is not toxic to dogs. The American Kennel Club (AKC) calls it a healthy treat in small amounts. It
has vitamin C, B vitamins, fiber, and minerals like manganese and copper.

The trouble comes from the parts around the flesh, and from eating too much. The next sections cover both.

## Which parts of pineapple should dogs avoid?

Do not give your dog the skin or the core. The AKC says the hard center core and the spiny skin can block a dog's
digestive tract. A blockage can be serious and may need surgery, so keep both parts in the trash, out of reach.

The leafy top is also spiky and tough, so keep that away too.

## How to serve pineapple safely

1. Cut off the skin and the top.
2. Cut out the hard core.
3. Slice the soft flesh into small, bite-sized pieces.
4. Offer a few chunks, and nothing else added.

Give it plain. No sugar, syrup, or other toppings.

## How much pineapple can a dog eat?

The AKC says a few chunks are usually enough for most dogs. Pineapple has a lot of natural sugar and fiber. Large
amounts can cause stomach trouble such as diarrhea.

The AKC also says sugary fruits should be limited for dogs who are overweight or have diabetes. If your dog has one of
these, ask your vet before you share pineapple.

Try a very small piece the first time. If your dog gets diarrhea or an upset stomach, stop.

## Other kinds of pineapple

### Can dogs eat canned pineapple?

No, it is best to skip it. The AKC says the syrup in canned fruit has too much sugar for most dogs' stomachs to handle.

### Can dogs eat frozen pineapple?

Yes. The AKC suggests frozen pieces of plain pineapple as a cool summer treat. Cut them small first so they are easy
to chew and swallow.

### What about dried pineapple and pineapple juice?

The sources we read do not cover dried pineapple or juice, so we can't say they are safe. Stick to fresh or frozen
plain pineapple, and ask your vet if you are unsure.

If your dog likes fruit, you can also share small amounts of [bananas](/can-dogs-eat-bananas/) or
[apples](/can-dogs-eat-apples/). Peel or prepare them the right way first. Fruit is a treat, so keep the amount
