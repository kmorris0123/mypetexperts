## How to choose a cat water fountain

### Do you need a fountain at all?

Not always. The Merck Veterinary Manual says to offer your cat more than one bowl of fresh, clean water and to
clean the bowls daily. It also says a fountain is an option, because cats are often drawn to moving water. Merck
notes that canned food adds water to your cat's diet too.

### Size and capacity

Capacity here ranges from 60 ounces to 168 ounces. A bigger tank means fewer refills and may suit a home with
more than one cat. A smaller fountain is easier to place. Look at the maker's dimensions before you buy.

### Material

- **Plastic:** The Catit Flower Fountain is made of BPA-free plastics, per the maker.
- **Stainless steel:** PetSafe calls its steel rust- and scratch-resistant.
- **Ceramic:** Pioneer Pet says ceramic bowls help prevent feline acne.

We found no veterinary source that ranks these materials, so pick based on cleaning and your cat.

### Filters, pumps and cleaning

Most fountains use a filter and a pump. Filters need regular changes, and pumps need cleaning. Pioneer Pet says to
clean its fountain weekly, change the filter every two to four weeks, and clean the pump monthly. PETLIBRO says to
replace its filter every two weeks. Check how often each filter changes and what it costs to replace.

### Power

Most fountains plug in. The PETLIBRO Dockstream 2 is cordless. PetSafe says the open bowl on its 360 model still
gives your cat water if the power goes out.

## Safety tips

- **Keep water fresh.** Refill and clean on the schedule your fountain's maker gives.
- **Keep a plain bowl too.** Merck advises more than one water bowl. A second spot gives your cat a choice.
- **Watch the cord.** Fountains that plug in have a cord. Keep it tucked away so your cat can't chew it.
- **Watch how much your cat drinks.** If your cat drinks and pees a lot, see your vet. Merck says this can point to an underlying problem.
- **Introduce it slowly.** Set the fountain next to the old bowl at first. Some cats take time to try something new.

For food that adds water to your cat's day, see [foods toxic to cats](/foods-toxic-to-cats/) before you share any
people food.
