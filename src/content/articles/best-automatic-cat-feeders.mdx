## How to choose an automatic cat feeder

### How often should your cat eat?

Cornell Feline Health Center says kittens under six months usually do best with three meals a day. From six months to a year, twice a day is generally best. Adult cats do well with one or two meals a day in most cases. Ask your vet what suits your cat. Our guide on [what kittens can eat](/can-kittens-eat-cat-food/) has more on kitten food.

### Wet food or dry food?

Check this first. Three of these feeders are for dry food only. The Cat Mate C500 and the SureFeed list wet food as compatible. Cornell notes that both wet and dry food are fine if the food is complete and balanced.

### Scheduled meals or an open bowl?

Cornell says free-feeding dry food can lead some cats to overeat and gain weight. A feeder with portion settings lets you control the amount. Measure the daily amount your vet suggests, and set the meals to add up to it.

### Power and Wi-Fi

App feeders need a Wi-Fi network. The PetSafe needs 2.4GHz. Look for a battery backup so meals still happen if the power goes out. The PetSafe and PETLIBRO list battery backups. Whisker lists an optional backup battery, sold separately.

### Capacity, kibble size and cleaning

Check the maximum kibble size and how many meals fit in the hopper. Also check what can go in the dishwasher. Whisker lists the bowl and hopper insert as dishwasher safe. PetSafe lists its bowl as top-rack safe.

## Safety tips

- **Test it while you're home.** Watch a few meals before you leave your cat alone with a new feeder.
- **Check the numbers.** Compare the portion size to your vet's advice and your cat's weight. For a cat on a diet, Cornell says weight should come off gradually, because sudden weight loss can cause serious problems. Work with your vet.
- **Watch your cat's habits.** If your cat stops eating or seems unwell, call your vet.
- **Keep fresh water out.** A feeder does not replace water. Cornell says fresh water should always be available, especially for cats eating dry food.
- **Clean it regularly.** Wash the bowl and any parts the maker says are safe to wash.
- **Refresh the food.** Cornell says food left out for grazing should be refreshed daily.
