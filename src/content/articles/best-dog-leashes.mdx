## How to choose a dog leash

### Pick the right length

The AKC says standard flat leashes are usually 4 to 6 feet long. Ruffwear notes that shorter leashes suit crowded areas, and longer ones suit running and hiking. Local leash laws differ. Six feet is common, so check the rules where you live.

### Match the leash to your dog's size

The AKC says small dogs do best with light leashes, while a thin strap or light clasp can fail on a large, strong dog. Big dogs also benefit from a padded handle. Always pick a sturdy metal clip.

### Choose a material

Nylon is tough, washable and inexpensive, according to the AKC. Leather is comfortable but needs special care. Chain resists chewing. The AKC also lists reflective materials, which help you be seen at night.

### Think about your walks

Ruffwear says hands-free leashes work well for running and hiking, while hand-held ones give better control of direction. Stretchy bungee leashes can soften sudden stops for dogs who often pause to sniff. Beginners should start with a standard 4 to 6 foot leash, per the AKC.

### What about retractable leashes?

The AKC advises against them for dogs that haven't learned to walk politely, because they reward pulling. We left them out of our picks for that reason. If you use one, the AKC says to choose a tape style over a thin cord, and use it only in open spaces, not on sidewalks or in crowded places.

## Safety tips

- **Check the clip and stitching often.** Replace a leash that is frayed or has a weak clasp.
- **Don't use a retractable leash in busy places.** The AKC warns that dogs can run into traffic or get hurt at the end of the line before you can step in.
- **Keep thin cords away from skin.** The AKC says thin leash cords can burn or cut if they wrap around people or dogs.
- **Use slip leads with care.** The AKC says they work best for trained dogs, so skip them for dogs still learning to walk on a leash.
- **Pair the leash with a well-fitted harness if your dog pulls.** See our guide to the [best no-pull dog harnesses](/best-no-pull-dog-harness/).
